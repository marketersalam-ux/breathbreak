BreathBreak

A breathing coach for screen workers

Take a breather that starts with how you're actually doing

BreathBreak uses your webcam to measure three things for one minute: your breathing rate, your pulse, and how often you blink. Then it guides you through a one-minute box-breathing exercise — and shows you the difference.

What the camera measures

  • Breathing — breaths per minute, from the small motion of your chest and shoulders.
  • Pulse — beats per minute, from tiny colour changes in your skin.
  • Blinks — blinks per minute, from your eyes. Screen work makes most of us blink far less than we should.

What happens to the data

  • Everything is processed on this computer. Nothing is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The camera starts only when you press Start measuring — never before.
  • No camera preview is shown, and no video ever leaves this page's server process.
  • The Presage SmartSpectra SDK contacts Presage only to check the API key. Telemetry is off.

How it works: 60 seconds of measuring, then an optional one-minute breather.

By pressing Start measuring you agree to use your camera for this session only.
